Proposal disclosure

Frequently asked questions

Understand the proposed approach, its benefits, and the considerations involved in community implementation.

What are the core strengths of the Impulse Sensor system?
It is preventive and real-time; creates a standardized evidence base that Town Councils currently lack (resolving disagreements over 10dB); it is cheap ($300/court) and scalable; it leaves court access open and hits upper-storey residents that barriers cannot shield.
What are the limitations of these strategies?
It requires explicit Town Council and HDB consent to install and maintain, depends heavily on reliable, calibrated sensors, and carries risks of free-riding or group credits being penalized by a single player's noise.
